## Scope, exclusions and M02 recommendations

Delivered M01 scope is Rust agent and Go control/relay/CLI skeletons, development
PostgreSQL/Compose, CI, config/security negatives and process/integration smoke,
as defined in the [execution contract](../M01_EXECUTION.md). The
[security baseline](../../security/README.md) explicitly excludes enrollment,
identity/credential storage, session authorization, TLS transport and remote SSH.
Healthy probes and valid configuration do not establish authentication. No
100-device load, CGNAT connectivity, successful SSH, production deployment,
dashboard or firmware/OTA capability is accepted here. Portable packages are
foundation previews; checksum integrity is not publisher authentication.

Recommendations below are planning inputs within [ADR-001](../adr/ADR-001-foundation.md)
and the [roadmap](../ROADMAP.md), not approved contracts or started implementation:

1. Close M01 human/hardware/governance gates and establish a separate M02
   execution plan with dependency-ordered issues, owners and measurable budgets.
2. Submit ADRs for device-bound enrollment/identity, per-session user/device
   authorization, expiry/rotation/revocation, offline/time semantics, versioned
   outbound TLS relay framing and SSH host-key verification. Wait for explicit
   human Tech Lead approval before implementing contract/security changes.
3. Before enabling transport, approve aggregate stream/buffer budgets, bounded
   reconnect/backoff and flash-write limits. Reserved maxima could otherwise
   multiply to 1 GiB; no such transport allocation exists in M01. Test deny,
   expired/revoked/wrong-device, replay, host-key mismatch and control outage cases.
4. Validate OpenSSH/Dropbear interoperability and real ARM resource/network
   profiles, with raw measurements and independent review. Preserve ordinary-user
   operation, relay inability to decrypt SSH, and no relay SSH private keys;
   introduce no custom shell protocol, auth bypass or unauthenticated forwarding.
5. Track deploy lint and supply-chain provenance/toolchain evaluation separately.
   Keep dashboard implementation in M03; early synthetic UX design follows the
   roadmap's review gates and does not establish connectivity.
